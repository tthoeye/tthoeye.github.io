      Open Calls
    </h1>

    <!-- INTRODUCTION -->
    <div style="margin-top:40px; font-size:18px; line-height:1.7;">
      <p>
        To bring ideas to life, <strong style="color:#1F75D6;">LDT4SSC</strong> is launching three rounds of open calls
        to fund and support <strong style="color:#29A329;">15–20 pilot projects across Europe</strong>.
        These pilots will be led by partnerships of cities, communities, businesses,
        researchers and other stakeholders working together on real-world challenges.
      </p>

      <p>
        The pilots will focus on critical areas such as
        <strong style="color:#29A329;">resource management</strong>,
        <strong style="color:#29A329;">climate resilience</strong>,
        <strong style="color:#29A329;">energy efficiency</strong> and more.
        Each pilot will develop AI-driven services tailored to local needs,
        using shared building blocks from the LDT4SSC Interoperability Blueprint.
      </p>

      <p>
        By connecting solutions across sectors and borders, the pilots will help create
        a <strong style="color:#1F75D6;">unified digital ecosystem</strong>.
        Local successes can be scaled up, replicated and shared across Europe.
      </p>

      <p>
        The result: a stronger digital foundation that empowers cities and communities to
        share resources, make smarter decisions and tackle common challenges —
        while promoting <strong>ethical AI</strong>, <strong>open standards</strong>
        and <strong>fair competition</strong> in line with Europe’s Digital Decade goals.
      </p>
    </div>
