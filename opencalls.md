    <h1 style="color:#1F75D6; font-size:42px; text-align:center;">Open Calls</h1>

    <!-- INTRODUCTION -->
    <div style="margin-top:40px; font-size:18px; line-height:1.6;">
      <p>
        To bring ideas to life, LDT4SSC is launching three rounds of open calls to fund and support
        <strong>15–20 pilot projects across Europe</strong>. These pilots will be led by partnerships of cities,
        communities, businesses, researchers and other stakeholders working together on real-world challenges.
      </p>

      <p>
        The pilots will focus on critical areas such as resource management, climate resilience,
        energy efficiency and more. Each pilot will develop AI-driven services tailored to local needs,
        while using shared building blocks from the LDT4SSC Interoperability Blueprint.
      </p>

      <p>
        By connecting solutions across sectors and borders, the pilots will help create a unified
        digital ecosystem. Local successes can be scaled up, replicated and shared across Europe.
      </p>

      <p>
        The result: a stronger digital foundation that empowers cities and communities to share resources,
        make smarter decisions and tackle common challenges — while promoting ethical AI,
        open standards and fair competition in line with Europe’s Digital Decade goals.
      </p>
    </div>
