    <!-- INTRODUCTION -->
    <div style="font-size:17px; line-height:1.7;">
      <p>
        To bring ideas to life, <strong style="color:#1F75D6;">LDT4SSC</strong> is launching three rounds of open calls
        to fund and support <strong style="color:#29A329;">15–20 pilot projects across Europe</strong>.
      </p>

      <p>
        These pilots will focus on critical areas such as
        <strong style="color:#29A329;">resource management</strong>,
        <strong style="color:#29A329;">climate resilience</strong> and
        <strong style="color:#29A329;">energy efficiency</strong>,
        developing AI-driven services tailored to local needs.
      </p>

      <p>
        By connecting solutions across sectors and borders, the pilots will help create
        a <strong style="color:#1F75D6;">unified digital ecosystem</strong> that can be scaled
        and replicated across Europe.
      </p>
    </div>
