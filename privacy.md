# 1\. About us

We are a diverse group of organisations united by a common goal: to deploy the Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities.

The Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities (LDT4SSC) Project maintains this website to enhance public access to information about its results and activities. Our goal is to keep this information updated. The Consortium has the right to interrupt service for internal reasons, e.g. maintenance, and is not obligated to make services available online without interruption.

The LDT4SSC consortium is composed of 11 partners (beneficiaries) across Europe, each bringing unique expertise and value to our project. The consortium is sustained through a number of supporting partners (Living-in EU sister networks) and related projects and initiatives.

**Collection and use of personal information**

**1\. When you browse our website**: <https://www.ldt4ssc.eu>

**Purpose**. We provide you with our project website, making sure it stays secure and up to date.

**Legal bases**. ​​(A) Your consent to receive our website. (B) Our legitimate interests to provide you with a secure website.

**Categories of personal data collected**. IP address, traffic data and personal information submitted by online forms.

- **Visitor Count**: The total number of visitors to our website, including unique visitors and pageviews. No personal information is collected.
- **Traffic Sources**: Information about where our visitors are coming from, such as direct traffic, search engines, or referral websites. No personal information is collected.
- **Popular Pages**: Insights into which pages on our website are the most visited. No personal information is collected.
- **Geographical Data**: The geographic locations of our website visitors. No personal information is collected.
- **Device Information**: Details about the devices (desktop, mobile, tablet) our visitors are using to access our site. No personal information is collected.
- **Browsing Behavior**: Information about how visitors navigate through our website, including the pages they visit and the time spent on each page. No personal information is collected.
- **Search Queries**: Information about what search terms are leading visitors to our website. No personal information is collected.

**Storage period**. We store your data from the day that you visit our website until the date of the end of the project (30 November 2028), unless you unsubscribe before that date, in which case we would delete your email address immediately.

**Processors**. OASC, ENoLL.

Other recipients of your personal data. None. We do not share any personal data with other recipients.

Transfers of personal data to third countries. None. We do not share any personal data with third countries.
