<!-- DESCRIPTION -->
<div style="background:rgba(41,163,41,0.12); padding:40px; border-radius:24px; margin-top:40px;">
<p>
The Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities (LDT4SSC) project, funded by the Digital Europe Programme (DEP),
invites applications for its second Open Call (Work Strand 1, 2 and 3).
</p>

<h2 style="color:#29A329;">What is Open Call 2?</h2>
<p>Open Call 2 invites applications for all three Work Strands.</p>
<p><strong>Work Strand 1:</strong> Inter-connection of existing Local Digital Twins to form a European-scale network.</p>
<p><strong>Work Strand 2:</strong> Creation of new Local Digital Twins based on shared challenges across cities and communities.</p>
<p><strong>Work Strand 3:</strong> Adding advanced AI-based capabilities and innovative open-source components to the EU LDT Toolbox.</p>
</div>

<!-- WHY APPLY -->
<h2 style="margin-top:70px; color:#29A329; text-align:center;">Why apply?</h2>

<div style="display:flex; flex-wrap:wrap; gap:20px; margin-top:30px;">

<div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #1F75D6;">
<strong>01. Interconnect & Federate</strong><br><br>
Link your Local Digital Twins with other European cities and communities to form a federated, interoperable network enabling
cross-border data exchange and multi-city optimisation.
</div>

<div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #29A329;">
<strong>02. Secure Financial Support</strong><br><br>
With a €17 million funding pot, de-risk innovation and deploy high-impact digital services without carrying the full financial burden.
</div>

<div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #F5B400;">
<strong>03. Access the EU LDT Toolbox</strong><br><br>
Use open-source specifications, reference architectures and certified AI tools that are standard-based and deployment-ready.
</div>

<div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #1F75D6;">
<strong>04. Deploy Advanced AI Services</strong><br><br>
Simulate future scenarios in areas like climate resilience and smart mobility before making real-world investments.
</div>

</div>
