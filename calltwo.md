<p>
The Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities (LDT4SSC) project, funded by the Digital Europe Programme (DEP), invites applications for its second Open Call (Work Strand 1, 2 and 3).
</p>

<h2 style="color:#29A329;">What is Open Call 2?</h2>
<p>Open Call 2 invites applications for all three Work Strands.</p>
<p><strong>Work Strand 1:</strong> Inter-connection of existing LDTs: demonstrates how existing Local Digital Twins can interconnect to form a European-scale network.</p>
<p><strong>Work Strand 2:</strong> Creation of LDTs based on common needs: creation of new Local Digital Twins designed around shared challenges affecting multiple cities and communities.</p>
<p><strong>Work Strand 3:</strong> Adding new and advanced AI capacities to the EU LDT Toolbox: develops advanced AI-based capabilities and innovative open-source components with immersive and predictive services.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="margin-top:60px; color:#29A329; text-align:center;">Why apply?</h2>

<div style="display:flex; flex-wrap:wrap; gap:20px; margin-top:20px; justify-content:center;">

  <div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #1F75D6;">
    <strong>01. Interconnect & Federate</strong><br><br>
    Move beyond isolated pilots. Our programme helps you link your existing Local Digital Twins with other European cities and communities.
    By adopting the Digital Europe blueprint, you will create a federated, interoperable network that enables cross-border data exchange,
    multi-city resource optimisation, and supports Europe’s technological sovereignty.
  </div>

  <div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #29A329;">
    <strong>02. Secure Financial Support</strong><br><br>
    De-risk your innovation. We provide direct financial support to help consortia develop and deploy complex services.
    With a total funding pot of €17 million, local authorities and partners can invest in high-impact digital transformation
    without carrying the full financial burden alone.
  </div>

  <div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #F5B400;">
    <strong>03. Access the EU Toolbox</strong><br><br>
    Gain access to the EU Local Digital Twin Toolbox — a repository of open-source specifications, reference architectures,
    and certified AI tools. Whether you are modelling traffic flows or energy grids, the framework ensures your solutions
    are compliant, standard-based, and ready for deployment.
  </div>

  <div style="flex:1; min-width:260px; background:#ffffff; padding:30px; border-radius:18px; box-shadow:0 10px 25px rgba(0,0,0,0.05); border-left:6px solid #1F75D6;">
    <strong>04. Deploy Advanced AI Services</strong><br><br>
    Turn data into predictive power. Pilots focus on cross-sectoral use cases — from climate resilience to smart mobility.
    Advanced AI models allow you to simulate future scenarios and test policy interventions virtually,
    before making real-world investments.
  </div>

</div>
