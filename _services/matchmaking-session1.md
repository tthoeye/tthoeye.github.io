Join the **LDT4SSC Matchmaking Session**, designed specifically for organisations wishing to connect with potential partners for the upcoming LDT4SSC Open Calls. 
This session is intended for organisations that **do not yet have a consortium** or who wish to **join an existing proposal**. 

**Date:** 18 December 2025 
**Time:** 11:30 AM – 1:00 PM CET 

During the session, you will be able to: 
- Meet organisations preparing WS1 proposals  
- Present your organisation’s expertise and interests 
- Identify missing roles for your consortium 
- Explore thematic clusters such as mobility, energy, environment, public services, resilience, and climate adaptation 
- Learn how to use the LDT4SSC Matchmaking Platform to publish your proposal and find partners 

**This event is open to all the potential applicants.**
We look forward to supporting you in building strong, complementary and impactful consortia for the LDT4SSC Open Calls!
