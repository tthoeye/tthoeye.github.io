The OCOVA 2026 International Forum, ‘Mountains of Tomorrow’, will take place in Les Orres on 3 February 2026, running from 9:00 am to 5:30 pm. 
Since 2004, the Forum has been the preferred meeting place for stakeholders in innovation, management and the development of mountain regions. 
It focuses on how advanced digital and communication technologies can contribute to regional development, with the aim of creating the economically efficient, socially integrated and environmentally responsible ‘mountains of tomorrow’.

The LDT4SSC project will be presented at the forum, promoting the WS2 and WS3 Open Calls in the context of ecological transition and territorial LDT use cases.
