  <h2 class="ldt-section-title">Concept</h2>

  <p>The LDT4SSC Matchmaking Event is an interactive online session designed to support potential applicants in preparing strong proposals for the Second Round of Open Calls (Work Strands 1, 2 and 3).</p>

  <p>The event aims to foster meaningful connections among cities, regions, technology providers, research organisations, SMEs and other stakeholders interested in Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities.</p>

  <p>Through structured pitches and a dedicated matchmaking session, participants will be able to present their expertise, explore complementarities and initiate discussions for consortium building.</p>

  <p>This event represents a key step in strengthening collaboration across Europe and accelerating the development of interoperable, scalable and AI-enabled Local Digital Twins.</p>

  <div class="ldt-divider"></div>

  <h2 class="ldt-section-title">Why join?</h2>

  <div class="ldt-box-light">
    <ul>
      <li>Gain a clear understanding of the Second Round of Open Calls (WS1, WS2 & WS3)</li>
      <li>Identify potential partners aligned with your technical focus and strategic objectives</li>
      <li>Present your organisation’s expertise, needs or project ideas through a short pitch</li>
      <li>Explore collaboration opportunities in a structured matchmaking setting</li>
      <li>Take concrete steps toward building competitive consortia before the 2 April 2026 deadline</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <p>Whether you are a city operating an existing Local Digital Twin, a community planning to deploy a new one, or a technology provider developing advanced AI solutions, this event will help you connect with the right partners!</p>

  <div class="ldt-divider"></div>

  <h2 class="ldt-section-title">Pitch your Pilot Project Proposal</h2>

  <div class="ldt-highlight">
    <p>During the Matchmaking Event, you will have the opportunity to deliver a short pitch (max. 3 minutes) focused on your pilot proposal.
      Use this moment to clearly present:
      <li>The core idea of your pilot project</li>
      <li>The specific partners or expertise you are looking for</li>
      <li>The concrete role they would play in your consortium</li>

    This is a practical, solution-oriented pitching session designed to accelerate consortium building and help you find the right match.
    A dedicated pitch template will be shared with registered participants who wish to present, ensuring clarity, consistency and an efficient session for everyone involved.
      </p>
  </div>
