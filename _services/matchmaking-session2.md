  <h2 style="color:#1F75D6;">Concept</h2>
  <p>The LDT4SSC Matchmaking Event is an interactive online session designed to support
potential applicants in preparing strong proposals for the Second Round of Open Calls
(Work Strands 1, 2 and 3).</p>
  <p>The event aims to foster meaningful connections among cities, regions, technology
providers, research organisations, SMEs and other stakeholders interested in Local Digital
Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities.</p>
  <p>Through structured pitches and a dedicated matchmaking session, participants will be able
to present their expertise, explore complementarities and initiate discussions for consortium
building.</p>
  <p>This event represents a key step in strengthening collaboration across Europe and
accelerating the development of interoperable, scalable and AI-enabled Local Digital Twins.</p>

  <div class="divider"></div>

  <h2 style="color:#1F75D6;">Why join?</h2>
  <div class="box">
    <ul>
      <li>Gain a clear understanding of the Second Round of Open Calls (WS1, WS2 & WS3)</li>
      <li>Identify potential partners aligned with your technical focus and strategic objectives</li>
      <li>Present your organisation’s expertise, needs or project ideas through a short pitch</li>
      <li>Explore collaboration opportunities in a structured matchmaking setting</li>
      <li>Take concrete steps toward building competitive consortia before the 2 April 2026 deadline</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <p>Whether you are a city operating an existing Local Digital Twin, a community planning to deploy a new one, or a technology provider developing advanced AI solutions, this event will help you connect with the right partners!</p>
