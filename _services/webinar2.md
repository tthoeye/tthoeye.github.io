Join us for an interactive webinar introducing the **open call for Work Strand 1** of the *Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities (LDT4SSC)* initiative on 25 November at 11:00-12:30.

This session is open to all stakeholders interested in strengthening the digital capabilities of cities and regions and joining a **federated European ecosystem of LDT-enabled communities**.

**Work Strand 1** focuses on connecting and enhancing existing Local Digital Twin platforms through **thematic clusters** addressing shared urban challenges: mobility, energy, environment, public services, resilience and climate adaptation. It supports communities with operational data platforms or early LDT implementations, helping them collaborate, exchange datasets and models and accelerate deployment.

Participants will gain a clear understanding of the **vision, objectives, technical requirements, funding structure and application process** for the open call, as well as how to access support via the **Matchmaking Platform** and **Helpdesk**.
