The recording and presentation slides from the LDT4SSC Webinar: Open Call – Work Strand 1 are now available. 

The session introduced the goals, technical requirements and funding structure of Work Strand 1, which aims to connect and strengthen existing Local Digital Twin platforms through thematic clusters such as mobility, energy, environment, public services, resilience and climate adaptation. Participants also received guidance on the application process and how to access support via the Matchmaking Platform and Helpdesk.
