    <h3>Description</h3>
    <p>Join us for an interactive Info Session introducing the 3rd and final round of open calls of the Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities (LDT4SSC) initiative. In this round, stakeholders will have the opportunity to apply to all three thematic pillars of the programme: Interconnecting existing Local Digital Twins (Work Strand 1), Deploying new Local Digital Twin services (Work Strand 2), or Deploying AI-based LDT services (Work Strand 3).</p>
    <p>This session is designed for any stakeholder interested in contributing to strengthen the digital capabilities of urban areas in the public domain and becoming part of a federated European ecosystem of Local Digital Twin (LDT)-enabled communities. The Info Session acts as an opportunity for potential applicants to learn about the call, the benefits of applying, and available support mechanisms — including tips on how to evolve from an application to a winning consortium.</p>
    <p>Participants will also have the chance to ask questions and engage directly with the project team throughout the session, including a dedicated 15-minute Q&amp;A. Whether you're an advanced adopter or just beginning your LDT journey, this session will help you understand how to get involved and which Work Strand you could align with most.</p>
