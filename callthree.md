#### Work Strand 3

## What is it about?

The **Local Digital Twins for Smart and Sustainable Communities (LDT4SSC)** project, funded by the **Digital Europe Programme (DEP)**, invites applications for its **first Open Call of Work Strand 3**.

It supports pilots that demonstrate how to add advanced AI services to the EU LDT Toolbox and deploy AI-based LDT services.


## What can you get?

- Up to **€1,000,000 per consortium**  
- **50% co-funding required**  
- Maximum **€500,000 per third party** across all strands  
- Project duration: **12–18 months**
